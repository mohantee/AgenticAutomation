MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2026
("Effective Date") by and between:

PARTY A: AgenticAutomation Inc., a Delaware corporation with principal offices at
100 Main Street, San Francisco, California 94102 ("Client")

PARTY B: Quantum AI Solutions Ltd., a company incorporated under the laws of the
State of New York with principal offices at 500 Park Avenue, New York, NY 10022
("Service Provider")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Service Provider possesses expertise in artificial intelligence and
document processing technologies; and

WHEREAS, Client desires to engage Service Provider to provide certain AI-powered
document processing and data extraction services;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the
parties agree as follows:

1. TERM
   This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period
   of two (2) years, expiring on January 14, 2028, unless earlier terminated in
   accordance with Section 8.

2. SERVICES
   Service Provider shall provide the following services:
   (a) Cloud-based document ingestion and processing infrastructure
   (b) AI model training and fine-tuning for document classification
   (c) Structured data extraction using foundation models
   (d) 24/7 monitoring and incident response

3. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT
   Service Provider guarantees:
   - 99.95% uptime for all production systems
   - Maximum response time of 5 seconds for document processing
   - Financial credits of 10% monthly fee for each 0.1% below SLA threshold

4. DATA PROCESSING
   - All data shall be processed within US-East regions
   - Service Provider is SOC2 Type II and GDPR compliant
   - Data retention period: 90 days after processing
   - Client data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3)

5. COMPENSATION
   Client shall pay Service Provider a total of $450,000.00 USD over the term,
   payable in equal monthly installments of $18,750.00 USD.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
   Client retains full ownership of all processed documents, extracted data, and
   any derivative works created from Client's data. Service Provider retains
   ownership of its proprietary AI models and processing infrastructure.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
   Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary
   information disclosed during the term of this Agreement.

8. TERMINATION
   Either party may terminate this Agreement:
   (a) With 90 days written notice to the other party
   (b) Immediately upon material breach that remains uncured for 30 days
   (c) Immediately upon the other party's insolvency or bankruptcy

9. GOVERNING LAW
   This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws
   of the State of Delaware, United States.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
